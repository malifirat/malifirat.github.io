Formula dashboard & enclosure

Cockpit hardware for the Olin Formula Team, including a dashboard and removable electronics enclosure designed for visibility, packaging, serviceability, and manufacturability.

SolidWorksFEADesign for manufacturing3D printingLaser cutting
TeamOlin Formula Team
AreaCockpit / drivetrain

TimelineSep 2024–May 2026

My contribution

I designed and fabricated the dashboard and electronics enclosure in SolidWorks. I used FEA to guide a design that reduced mass by about 20% and refined mounting and connector packaging through reviews with the electrical and chassis teams.

Design decisions

The dashboard included a sun guard above the LEDs to improve visibility in direct light. The electronics enclosure used a removable lid so components could be serviced without replacing the whole housing.

Mounting points, enclosure dimensions, airflow features, heat sinks, and connector clearances were adjusted as surrounding electrical and chassis requirements evolved.

Manufacturing

The parts were designed around laser-cut flat panels and 3D-printed features. The design process emphasized access, mounting stiffness, connector clearance, assembly sequence, and practical fabrication constraints.
