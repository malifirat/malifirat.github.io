Olin Formula Team · 2024–2026

Formula Dashboard & Electronics Enclosure

A dashboard and electronics enclosure designed for direct-sun visibility, maintenance access, tight packaging, and real fabrication constraints.

Project

Overview

I designed the Formula SAE vehicle dashboard and electronics enclosure, nicknamed the “Nostril,” as part of the Olin Formula Team. The work required iterative CAD, cross-team reviews, and adaptation to changing electrical and chassis requirements.

Design goals & constraints

  • Improve LED visibility in sunlight.
  • Provide fast maintenance and debugging access.
  • Package the sensing controller, throttle, brakes, Raspberry Pi, HAT, and connectors.
  • Avoid steering-wheel and chassis interference.
  • Design for laser cutting, welding, and 3D printing.

Dashboard

The dashboard used an angled front face and a sun guard above the LED indicators. Mounting was strengthened after design review feedback showed that the initial support strategy was insufficient.

Electronics enclosure

The enclosure used a removable top and tabbed geometry for service access, connector packaging, and future board space. Chassis-mounted interfaces and retention features were designed around real assembly and maintenance needs.

Iteration

The design changed through DR1/DR2 and cross-team reviews: dashboard mounts increased, enclosure dimensions changed with connector requirements, and access strategy was revised based on maintenance feedback.

My role

  • Led CAD design for the dashboard and enclosure.
  • Defined mechanical interfaces and access strategy.
  • Integrated feedback from electrical and chassis teams.
  • Prepared designs for manufacturing and prototyping.
