How I work

I am most interested in engineering where analysis connects directly to something that gets built, tested, and improved.

Design through iteration

Across projects, I use CAD and modeling to form a first design, test it against the real system, and use the failures or mismatches to guide the next iteration. That loop shows up in the HydroFleet anchoring mechanism, Formula packaging, Rocky controller development, and manufacturing work at Cibo Vita.
